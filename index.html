FAQ

Questions people ask before they connect an account

Does FinSmart use AI to categorize my spending?

No. Every category, forecast, and alert comes from rules you set or adjust yourself, so you always know why a number moved.

Is my financial data stored securely?

Yes. Your data is encrypted and kept private — FinSmart doesn't sell or share it with third parties.

Can I connect my bank accounts?

You can import transaction history securely or log expenses manually — whichever keeps your numbers most accurate.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Switch plans or cancel whenever you like — there's no lock-in period.

Does it work on mobile?

The dashboard is fully responsive, so budgets, alerts, and forecasts work the same on your phone.
